## Analysis Summary

Customers aged 36-50 spend the most overall, making them the most valuable group.
Income has some effect on CLV, but how often a customer buys matters more.

The top 10% of customers tend to buy frequently rather than spend big each time,
so keeping existing customers engaged (loyalty programs, discounts) would work
better than trying to get people to spend more per purchase.

The 18-25 group has the lowest CLV mostly because they earn and spend less.
Targeting them with budget-friendly offers early on could help build long-term loyalty.
